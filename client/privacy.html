Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and why. Last updated: September 2026.

What we DON'T collect

  • 🎥 Your calls are never recorded. Video and audio travel peer-to-peer between you and your partner.
  • 📍 We don't track your precise location.
  • 💰 We don't sell your data. Ever.

What we do collect

  • Chat messages — relayed through our server so they (and translations) can be delivered. We don't keep them longer than needed.
  • Google login info (only if you log in) — your name, email and profile photo, to identify your account.
  • Reports — when you report someone, we log it to keep the community safe.
  • Basic technical data — like online counts and error logs, to keep the service running.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie (pandoo_sid) to remember your Google login. No advertising trackers.

Your control

Log out anytime to end your session. For data questions, reach us via the Contact page.
