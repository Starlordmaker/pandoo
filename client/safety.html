Safety & Community Guidelines

Pandoo is for friendly conversations. Keep it that way. 💛

Hard rules

  • 🔞 18+ only. If you're under 18, Pandoo isn't for you yet.
  • 🚫 No nudity or sexual content — ever. You'll be removed.
  • 🤬 No harassment, hate speech or threats.
  • 📢 No spam, scams or ads.
  • 🎣 Never share passwords, bank details or personal info with strangers.

Tools that protect you

  • Report — flag anyone breaking the rules, right from the call screen.
  • Next / Skip — uncomfortable? One tap and you're matched with someone new.
  • Spam filters — abusive chat behavior is blocked automatically.

Stay smart

Strangers are strangers. Don't share your address, school, workplace or financial details. If something feels wrong, end the chat and report it.
